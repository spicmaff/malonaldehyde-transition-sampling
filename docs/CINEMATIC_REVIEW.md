validated optimization enhancement is ready. Missing or mismatched JSON leaves
all old and new controls safely disabled. The atlas supports native buttons plus
Left/Right/Home/End. The same selected family is conveyed by text, pressed state,
and outline, not color alone. Static state values remain readable without JS.

play buttons keep their accessible names synchronized with their visible labels.
