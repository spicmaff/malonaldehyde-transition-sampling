## Availability boundary

Public data, QC, own verifiers and export manifest allow offline numerical
reproduction. Models are identified by exact SHA; external MLIP/QE executables and
pseudopotential binaries are not redistributed. Source/private path prefixes are
replaced by declared aliases without numeric changes. Re-execution of physical
models/DFT is outside the compact reproducer. No fabricated DOI, journal or release.
